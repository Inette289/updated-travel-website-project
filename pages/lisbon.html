Lisbon | Portugal

Lisbon, the coastal capital of Portugal, is known for its rich history, colorful neighborhoods, historic trams, and vibrant nightlife. It's a city of cultural landmarks, scenic viewpoints, and delicious cuisine.

Food lovers will enjoy fresh seafood, traditional pastries such as Pastéis de Belém, and wine tasting in local taverns. Lisbon also serves as a gateway to nearby gems such as Sintra and Cascais.

Travel Guide:

Key Areas

  • Alfama: Historic streets and Fado music
  • Bairro Alto: Nightlife and restaurants
  • Belém: Monuments and famous pastries

Key Travel Information

  • Best Time to Visit: Spring (March – May) and Fall (September – October)
  • Entry Requirements: Schengen Visa required for non-EU travelers
  • Currency: Euro (EUR)
  • Safety: Generally safe; watch for pickpockets in tourist areas
  • Culture: Respect local customs; tip 5–10% in restaurants

Top Destinations & Activities

  • Belém: Jerónimos Monastery, Belém Tower, Pastéis de Belém
  • Alfama: Narrow streets, Fado performances, Lisbon Cathedral
  • Baixa & Chiado: Shopping, squares, and cafes
  • Parque das Nações: Modern architecture, Oceanarium
  • Sintra (day trip): Palaces, castles, and gardens

Budgeting (Per Person / Week):

  • Budget: $400 – $800 USD
  • Mid-Range: $800 – $2000 USD
  • Luxury: $3000+ USD

Getting Around

Efficient trams, buses, metro, and walking in compact neighborhoods. Taxis and rideshares are also available.

Tips

  • Wear comfortable shoes for hilly streets
  • Try local food like Pastéis de Nata and seafood dishes
