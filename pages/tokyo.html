Tokyo | Japan

Tokyo is a thrilling metropolis where the ancient and modern coexist seamlessly, offering a cityscape that ranges from centuries-old temples and serene gardens to cutting-edge skyscrapers and neon-lit streets. Every neighborhood tells a story—from the historic streets of Asakusa to the fashion-forward energy of Harajuku, the luxury shopping of Ginza, and the bustling nightlife of Shinjuku.

Visitors can immerse themselves in rich cultural traditions, savor world-class cuisine, explore futuristic technology hubs, or enjoy tranquil escapes in nearby parks and gardens. Tokyo’s vibrant festivals, art galleries, and entertainment districts ensure there’s always something new to discover. Whether you’re a cultural enthusiast, foodie, shopper, or adventure seeker, the city promises unforgettable experiences and a journey that blends heritage with innovation.

Travel Guide

Top Attractions & Experiences

  • Historic & Cultural Sites: Senso-ji Temple, Meiji Shrine, Imperial Palace, Asakusa district
  • Modern Landmarks: Tokyo Skytree, Shibuya Crossing, Roppongi Hills, Odaiba waterfront
  • Neighborhoods & Experiences:
    • Harajuku for cutting-edge fashion
    • Shinjuku for bustling nightlife and skyscraper views
    • Ginza for luxury shopping and fine dining
    • Akihabara for anime, gaming, and electronics
  • Culinary Highlights: Sushi at Tsukiji/Toyosu Market, ramen in Ikebukuro, street food along Asakusa, themed cafés
  • Day Trips & Nature Escapes: Mount Fuji, Nikko, Kamakura, Hakone hot springs, coastal towns near Chiba

Best Time to Visit

  • Spring (March to April): Cherry blossoms create iconic scenic views, with mild temperatures ideal for sightseeing
  • Autumn (September to November): Comfortable weather, vibrant foliage, and fewer tourists
  • Summer (June to August): Hot and humid, but festivals and fireworks add unique experiences
  • Winter (December to February): Cooler temperatures, fewer crowds, and seasonal illuminations

Practical Information

  • Visa: Many nationalities require a visa; check Japan’s official travel requirements
  • Currency: Japanese Yen (¥)
  • Electricity: Type A and B plugs (100V, 50/60Hz)
  • Recommended Duration: 5 to 10 days for city exploration and nearby attractions
  • Connectivity: Free Wi-Fi available in major stations and districts; consider a pocket Wi-Fi or SIM card for uninterrupted mobile access

Travel Tips

  • Getting Around: Tokyo Metro and JR lines are fast, reliable, and clean. Use IC cards like Suica or Pasmo for convenience
  • Safety: Tokyo is exceptionally safe; pickpocketing is rare, but stay aware in crowded areas
  • Dining: From Michelin-starred restaurants to street vendors, Tokyo offers endless culinary options. Try sushi, ramen, tempura, and traditional sweets
  • Culture & Etiquette: Bowing is common; shoes are removed in temples and some restaurants
  • Language: Japanese is official; English is widely understood in tourist zones and transport signage
