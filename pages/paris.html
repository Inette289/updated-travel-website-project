Paris | France

Paris is a top global destination known for iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame, best visited during spring (April-June) or fall (September-November) for ideal weather.

Visitors should explore neighborhoods like Montmartre and Le Marais, indulge in French cuisine, and use the extensive Metro system. The city requires a Schengen visa for many, with the Euro as currency.

Travel Guide:

Top Attractions & Experiences

  • Iconic Landmarks: Eiffel Tower, Arc de Triomphe, Notre-Dame Cathedral
  • Museums & Art: The Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou
  • Neighborhoods & Views: Montmartre (Sacré-Cœur), Champs-Élysées, Seine River cruise, Luxembourg Gardens
  • Day Trips: Palace of Versailles, Disneyland Paris

Best Time to Visit

  • April to June & September to November: Mild weather and fewer crowds
  • July to August: Hot and busy; August quieter as locals travel
  • December: Festive season with Christmas markets

Practical Information

  • Visa: Schengen visa required for many; US citizens visa-free up to 90 days
  • Currency: Euro (€)
  • Electricity: Type C and E plugs (230V, 50Hz)
  • Recommended Duration: 5 days to 2 weeks

Travel Tips

  • Getting Around: Metro is fast and reliable; walking recommended for neighborhoods
  • Safety: Remain vigilant in crowded areas
  • Dining: Cafés, patisseries, and Le Marais falafel for local flavor
  • Language: French official; English widely understood in tourist areas
