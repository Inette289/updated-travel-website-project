Bali | Indonesia

Bali is a premier Indonesian destination offering a mix of sandy beaches, vibrant nightlife, lush rice paddies, and ancient temples. The best time to visit for dry weather is between April and October, with May to September being ideal.

Travel Guide:

Key Areas

  • Seminyak: Shopping and dining
  • Ubud: Culture and rice terraces
  • Uluwatu: Surfing and clifftop views

Key Travel Information

  • Best Time to Visit: Dry season (April – October); rainy season (November – March)
  • Entry Requirements: Most travelers need an e-Visa on Arrival (e-VOA) and arrival card
  • Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)
  • Safety: Watch for petty crime, traffic risks, and drink spiking
  • Culture: Respect local traditions; improper behavior can lead to deportation

Top Destinations & Activities

  • Ubud: Rice terraces, yoga studios, waterfalls
  • Seminyak & Kuta: Beaches, shopping, nightlife
  • Uluwatu: Clifftop temples and surfing
  • Nusa Dua: Luxury, family-friendly resorts
  • Nusa Penida & Gili Islands: Snorkeling, diving, boat trips

Budgeting (Per Person / Week)

  • Budget: $500 – $1000 USD
  • Mid-Range: $1000 – $3000 USD
  • Luxury: $5000+ USD

Getting Around

Renting scooters is popular but risky. Safer options include private drivers, taxis (Grab/Gojek), and walking.

Tips

  • Check vaccinations; rabies risk exists
  • Pack light clothing, sunscreen, and a light rain jacket
